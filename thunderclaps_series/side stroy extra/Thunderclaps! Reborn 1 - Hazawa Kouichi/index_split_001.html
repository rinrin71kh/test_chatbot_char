Chapter 1: Parasite Begins

 

Long lines wound their way around Shineland 60, the biggest landmark of Ikebukuro, Toshima, Tokyo. The people in charge had divided the thousands of waiting people into 4 different lines.

Guards in what looked like fast food uniforms walked in the space between the lines and politely cautioned the people to stay in an orderly line.

The female idol group Miracle Waffle was having a CD handout in Shineland 60’s event space today. The group had a new CD going on sale and the idols would be directly handing out copies to fans, so it was like a dream come true for those fans.

About 2/3 of the people in the lines were young men, but there were still a lot of female fans. Almost everyone was glittering with Miracle Waffle merch, including badges bearing the idols’ faces. There were a fair number of girls dressed up in the flashy but cute stage outfits with excitement in their differently-sized chests, and the highly-unlikely fantasies so commonly found in fans were blossoming in their minds.

The scent of youth and a passionate heat hung over the colorful lines, but not everyone there was Japanese. There were a fair number of foreigners who had become fans watching Japanese idol videos online and started flying to Japan just to participate in the events and go to the concerts.

Four foreigners were among the fans who had arrived at Shineland 60 especially early. One was a blonde, blue-eyed white girl in her upper teens and the other three were adult women.

The adults were a black-haired white woman, a Black woman, and a Hispanic woman and they were all above the average age of a Miracle Waffle fan. But their shirts and skirts bore the same idol products and badges as the young girl.

They were discussing Miracle Waffle’s appeal in English when a male guard patrolling between the lines casually approached them.

The three women immediately gathered around the blonde girl and glared sharply at the guard.

The muscular guard spoke in Japanese with a loud, carrying voice similar to an opera singer.

“You are Linda Burns, daughter of the US Secretary of State currently visiting the country, and her bodyguards, aren’t you? I am a field agent for the Enforcers of Evil. My job is to kill you gruesomely and publicly as a message to your father.”

The instant he mentioned the killing of the VIP girl, the three bodyguards disguised as idol fans drew their guns and opened fire on the guard without waiting for any kind of signal. They were each less than a meter from the man – far too close to miss. Not one of the bullets strayed from its target.

The macho guard did nothing to stop the gunfire and calmly let the bullets hit him. The bullets tore through his uniform but were easily repelled by the sturdy body within. He was not wearing any kind of bulletproof armor. Even the bullets that hit him between the eyes, on the cheek, and on the nose left not even a scratch before falling around his cream-colored shoes.

After the brief roar of gunfire, screams erupted from the crowd and they all began to run away.

The guard did not even glance in the direction of the fleeing crowd, keeping his back to the screams and thundering footsteps and placing an arrogant smile on his lips.

“You will find resistance and escape are both quite impossible. Surely the US government’s bodyguards are aware the Enforcers of Evil only hire Offbeat field agents.”

Some in the fleeing crowd were shouting “He’s an Offbeat!” or “It’s an Offbeat criminal!”

Offbeat was the general term for anyone with a wide variety of superhuman powers.

During the Cold War, a superhuman calling himself Super Offbeat made a public appearance after saving Washington DC from a nuclear terrorist attack. From that point on, countless superhumans had started operating in public. They had all gained their powers in different ways and they used them for different purposes, but people still called them Offbeats after the first to make himself known.

“My client asked me not to kill anyone but my target, so the rest of you can feel free to spread the video of the Secretary of State’s daughter’s killing all over Twitter and Facebook. Now, how would you like to die, Linda? I recommend you and your bodyguards get to know each a lot better with your brains and guts splattered together on the asph- gwah!”

A white shape raced down toward the macho man like a bullet and a white boot kicked him in the shoulder. The body that had repelled a hail of close-range gunfire was knocked backwards and brought to its knees in an area quite deserted now that the crowd had fled.

The white shape that had kicked him away landed in front of the Secretary of State’s daughter and bodyguards to protect them all.

“That’s enough! Surrender now!”

The courageous voice ringing in front of the skyscraper was followed by the girl behind her shouting her name with hope in her voice.

“Flare! The precious Japanese superhero!”

A lot of Offbeats misused their superpowers for their own personal gratification, but a lot of others had followed Super Offbeat’s example and chosen to protect and save people with their powers. They were called superheroes after the comic book characters.

Flare was one of the superheroes who operated in Japan.

Her real name was Hyuga Kirara. That identity was of course kept secret from the public, but she had actually been created by an evil genius scientist just a few years prior. She had been fortunate enough to escape her creator and had become a hero.

She was only a few years old, but she looked around 20 and her courageously beautiful face was outlined by hair she kept short.

Her splendid proportions were covered by a white miniskirt costume reminiscent of a cheerleader and her slender legs were contained in long white boots. Her large chest bore a yellow flame symbol and a hip-length white cape fluttered behind her back.

The kicked macho man glared confidently at her with a daring smile on his lips.

“So you’re Flare, huh? You’re from that Thunderclaps superhero team that’s been getting real popular in Tokyo of late, aren’t you?”

“The Enforcers of Evil are mercenaries only in it for the money. You have no principles of your own, so why bother fighting a superhero when you don’t have to?”

“Being in it for the money makes completing our job no matter what our driving principle!”

He stood up with beautiful rainbows bursting from his back and taking the shape of butterfly wings. A rainbow laser also fired from his eyes. The beam grazed Flare’s side, burned a hole in the edge of her cape, and flew toward Linda behind her.

A plain look of “uh, oh” formed on Flare’s face, but the rainbow beam froze midflight before it could reach the bodyguards standing in front of Linda.

An emerald-shining silhouette descended from above them and came to a stop in midair. Linda gave another joyous cry with some kind of invisible barrier protecting her.

“Rose Device of the Thunderclaps! The brilliant Japanese superhero!”

“I have placed a barrier around Linda-san and her bodyguards. All physical and chemical attacks are meaningless now.”

A calm and beautiful woman’s voice came from the shiny green armor covering Rose Device’s entire body. It covered her so thoroughly that her face, hair, and every bit of skin was hidden from view. But the armor itself reproduced the proportions of an adult woman, forcing anyone who viewed it to imagine how attractive the wearer was.

A bright red rose relief blossomed on the smooth curves of the chest, providing an elegant accent that drew the eye. That too gave her an adult look.

Which was why no one would suspect that the armor was occupied by Kitahara Shizuko, a small, slender girl of 18. Only her Thunderclaps teammates knew.

When she was young, Shizuko had been caught in an accident from one of her scientist father’s experiments, damaging her brain and all her nerves beyond repair. Her father had injected her body with the nanomachines he was developing, had them replace her brain and nerves, and successfully preserved her personality and memories.

Shizuko was a unique cyborg in that her brain was mechanical and her body was biological. She now wore the armor she had developed herself, linked it directly to her brain, and fought as a superhero.

The emerald green metal mask glared at the butterfly man.

“I have found a match in my database. You have changed your appearance, but you go by the codename Rainbow Butterfly. You are a veteran member of the Enforcers of Evil. You can no longer harm Linda-san, so please surrender.”

Rainbow Butterfly seemed to obey Rose Device since he did not fire any more rainbow beams from his eyeballs, but a stone wall several meters tall suddenly rose up behind the green armor. The thick wall bent at a right angle before Rose Device could react and crashed into the back of her armor.

Her defense system activated, so the deadly impact from so much mass did not reach the slender human body within. But she could not avoid having her armor slammed down into the asphalt.

“Ahhh!?”
Her armor’s sensors told her that wall had not been there just a moment before.

(Was the wall teleported there? No, was it a power specifically for controlling stone walls?)

The questions came to mind while more and more stone walls rose all around her and collapsed onto her like multiple avalanches from all directions. Her armor was soon pinned below all that mass. It was simple violence, but it was still devastatingly powerful.

“Rose!” shouted Flare.

She tried to run to her teammate, but Rainbow Butterfly grabbed her arms and pinned them behind her back. There was a grin on the rugged face that didn’t match his elegant codename and his seven-colored glowing wings flapped fiercely.

Flare and Rainbow Butterfly shot straight up like a rocket and soon vanished from view of those on the surface.

More stone walls appeared and pushed toward Linda and her bodyguards. The four were defenseless and would soon be squashed like a mosquito caught between a pair of swatting hands, but the walls were blocked by an invisible barrier and could not cause their harm.

Rose Device’s voice came from below the mountain of walls.

“This will not work either. The barrier surrounding Linda-san and her bodyguards will continue to operate on its own even if you bury me alive.”

She had already located the master of the walls. She had launched several ladybug-sized drones from her armor and their cameras were transmitting their video feeds directly into her brain.

Who knows where he found it, but the master of the walls was wearing a yellow outfit based on those worn by Chinese court officials. He looked like he had stepped off the set of a Chinese history film.

Rose Device instantly performed a search of her brain’s database. His codename was Great Wall, but he wasn’t Chinese – just obsessed with Chinese history. He was an Offbeat criminal only recently recognized by the world’s justice systems, so the mechanism of his power to cause stone walls to appear and move around was still unknown. He must have joined the underworld mercenaries of the Enforcers of Evil at some point.

(They’re just rocks!)

Rose Device increased her armor’s output to wield the strength of a large crane and lift the stone walls piled on top of her. They slid heavily away from her.

But more and more walls would appear and fall on her instead. The pile of walls was soon even bigger than before.

“You fool! I can create an unlimited number of walls! Will your precious barrier remain after I crush you and your armor flat? Nwa ha ha ha ha ha!”

Great Wall demonstrated the maniacal laughter and exaggerated gesturing of a cheesy actor, but he failed to notice something like a bluish-white serpent coiling through the air behind him.

He continued cackling until the blue snake touched his neck.

“Gwah!”

His entire body stiffened and then convulsed. He had been shocked by a powerful electric attack. The endless spawning of walls stopped. The ones moving like animals to attack Rose Device came to a stop and became out-of-place works of art.

The electricity wrapped around his neck vanished and he turned around on unsteady legs with his hair standing on end like a manga character.

A woman in a red and blue costume stood there.

Her formfitting red costume bore a blue lightning bolt pattern, but the thighs of both legs were transparent, giving it the appearance of a high-leg one-piece swimsuit worn with long boots.

That costume revealed the womanly shape of her body. Her large breasts, thin waist, high butt, and thick legs were all sensually beautiful.

Similar to her thighs, the center of the costume had a clear portion in the shape of a lightning bolt from the chest down to below the navel, so her cleavage was left entirely exposed

She left little to the imagination with her sexy body, but she wore a red eye mask to hide the area around her eyes. Still, the angled eyes, long nose, and shapely lips were enough to know she was a beautiful, mature woman.

Her hair was worn in a single rope-like braid that reached the center of her back, which made for an amusing mismatch with her adult face.

She spoke before Great Wall could react to her.

“Oh, dear. I assumed you relied on your superpowers, but you have a surprisingly fit body.”

Partway through her comment, she spun on her left leg, surrounded the toe of her right leg’s red boot with crackling electricity, and slammed it into the side of Great Wall’s head. Then she switched to her other leg, made another half rotation and slammed the electrified toe of her left boot into the side of his head as well.

“Gwuh.”
He gave a short grunt as his face slammed into the stone walls he had gathered here himself. He shook like a flopping fish just once and then ceased moving.

After landing her high kicks, the woman stopped with both legs together and let out a deep breath like a martial artist at the end of a demonstration.

Linda had simply stared at the quick work, so she only now managed to get her voice out.

“Star Thunder, leader of the Thunderclaps! The amazing Japanese superhero!”

Star Thunder’s real name was Rindou Rei. She was 26 and the private life of the face hidden below the red eye mask was of course a secret.

Rei was born to an alien father and an earthling mother. Her ability to emit electricity from her body and control it was a mutant power created by the combination of two species from different planets.

Star Thunder waved to the girl and women inside the barrier.

“Don’t worry. I promise we will protect you.”

Then she used the communicator in her right ear to speak to Rose Device who was still buried below so many walls.

“Are you alright?”

“I am. I will be out before long. So was Great Wall the last of the assassins?”

“Ocelot said she still sensed something, so stay on alert.”

Before Star Thunder was even done speaking, a manhole lid shot up from the asphalt nearby and something truly odd appeared.

It was a mass of countless human arms.

Some were the muscular arms of men, some the slender arms of women, and some the tiny arms of children, but they were all lifeless, pale, covered in severe wounds, showing discolored flesh, and dripping with blackened blood.

“There they are!”

Star Thunder sent electricity surging from her hands and a horizontal bolt of lightning crashed into the bizarre arm creature.

But the electricity brought no change to the arms. Even as more lightning attacks struck them, they stretched out like rubber to approach the barrier.

The barrier could only be expressed through mathematical formulae, not with words, and Rose Device was the only one here who could comprehend it. The many creepy fingers contacted the invisible barrier that had blocked a destructive beam and so many stone walls. Rose Device was confident it could stop any form of matter or energy.

“No way!”

Her many drone cameras showed her the fingers and hands passing through the barrier like it didn’t even exist. They easily slipped through and approached Linda.

Linda screamed and her bodyguards formed a wall in front of her. The three women had been trained to die as a human shield if necessary, but the fear was plain to see on their faces.

They understood on an instinctual level, rather than a rational or intellectual one, that they were about to experience something far more horrifying than the death they had been prepared to accept. They knew this would be a fate worse than death. That they didn’t scream and flee had more to do with the fear numbing their bodies and locking up their legs than with their faithfulness to their mission.

The arms stopped just before their pale fingertips touched the women’s stiffened faces. And like a video playing in reverse, the injured arms moved backwards and disappeared down the manhole.

A moment later, a yellow shape leaped from the manhole, curled up in midair, and flipped around before landing on the asphalt on two feet.

It was a bestial woman.

Her body was shaped like a human woman with large breasts and a bold shape to the butt. But that body was covered by animal fur that gave her a bright yellow coat with black spots.

The triangular animal ears on top of her head were truly ears and not just part of her hair and a long tail colored with yellow and black rings grew from above her butt.

She had a yellow mane around her head and she had the narrow vertical pupils of a cat.

She wasn’t wearing anything artificial like clothing, shoes, or accessories. It was hard to say for sure with all the fur, but she was often suspected of actually being nude.

“Ocelot of the Thunderclaps!” shouted Linda.

That hero name came from a wild cat found in the jungles of South America. Her pre-transformation name was Yanagi Isabella Mika and she was 18.

“The fabulous Japanese super-”

Linda’s words suddenly vanished, replaced by a scratchy gasp of terror.

A man appeared from the manhole after Ocelot. He was a bald man of indeterminate age with sunken cheeks. He was wearing a black suit from a famous luxury brand. His tie was also black, making it look like mourning clothes, but it was all filthy after being in the manhole.

But no one cared about his face or his clothing. The superheroes, Linda, and the bodyguards were all focused on the many people who appeared around him.

Dozens of people spread out on either side of the bald man. Their sexes, ages, and clothing were all different, but they were all pallid, mortally wounded, and leaking bluish-black blood.

Only Ocelot’s cheerful voice broke the icy atmosphere.

“He goes by the name of Ghost Tamer and science can’t handle him. He uses the spirits of the dead, so this is a job for a shaman like me!”

Mika had been accepted by the great spirits of South America and had become one of the leading shamans on the continent. She transformed into a bestial feline form with secret arts taught to her by those spirits, not with any kind of technology.

Ocelot’s tail stood straight up and her sharp-clawed feet kicked off the asphalt as she dove into the mass of dead spirits.

Ghost Tamer commanded the dead with an arrogant and gloomy smile.

“My faithful and invincible vengeful spirits, I command you to tear this foolish beast limb from limb and add her to your ranks.”

The dead seemed to slide silently across the asphalt as they pressed in around Ocelot. Just as it looked like she had been swallowed up, a South American anaconda appeared from each of her arms.

They were hard to see through her fur, but she had sacred tattoos on the backs of her hands and those had summoned a pair of anaconda spirits.

Biological anacondas were said to reach 9 meters at the most, but the spirits had no such limits. The two anacondas crawled through the air too fast for the eye to follow and coiled around the dozens of dead spirits. Rose Device’s super-science barrier and Star Thunder’s electricity had failed to capture them, but the serpents did the trick.

The dead’s arms struggled in vain as they failed to move any further forward.

This must have been a first for Ghost Tamer because his eyes widened and he shouted in panic.

“What did you do!? Nothing can stop a vengeful spirit!”

“These aren’t vengeful spirits!” angrily shouted back Ocelot with the dead all around her. “They died ordinary deaths and should have found eternal peace with no earthly regrets, but then you damaged their spiritual forms and forcibly transformed them into your slaves! You won’t get away with this repulsive desecration of the dead!”

A roar carrying great spiritual power rattled the air and Ghost Tamer’s face and scalp shook.

“I’ll give you all the peace you deserve, so wait just a bit, okay?”

“Fucking catgirl. You’ll pay for this!”

Realizing he was in trouble, Ghost Tamer left some childish parting words, turned tail, and ran.

Ocelot jumped high over the dead’s heads, performed a midair flip, and landed on the back of Ghost Tamer’s filthy mourning clothes. She pinned him to the asphalt and the fall pushed a voiceless breath from his mouth.

Ocelot grabbed his collar with her right hand while staying on top of his back.

“Right now, I’m not a superhero, protector of the living. I’m a shaman, protector of the dead. You need to be punished for disturbing the order of death!”

She spread out the fingers of her left hand and grabbed his face. The sharp feline claws growing from the tips of those fingers dug into his forehead and cheeks.

“L-let go of me! Ahhhh!”

Black wriggly things emerged from the palm pressed against his nose and mouth.

“These are Amazon leech spirits. They’ll enter your body and drain all your blood from the inside. Let’s see how you like being a dried husk of a corpse.”

“Eeeeeee…”

Ghost Tamer’s scream quickly faded away into nothingness as the wriggling leeches filling his mouth blocked up his throat. They also forced their way in through his nostrils, earholes, and the gaps around his eyes. His leech-covered face contorted with terror, stiffening and tensing until he finally passed out.

All of the leeches immediately returned to Ocelot’s left hand.

“Heh, I was just scaring you a little. A superhero like me wouldn’t actually kill someone. I have to obey human laws outside the jungle. Oh!”

Ocelot’s feline ears moved around, picking up a sound from the sky. Star Thunder and Rose Device, who had extracted herself from the walls, looked up into the sky too.

A small dot in the blue sky quickly grew larger until it became Flare and Rainbow Butterfly grappling as they dropped. Star Thunder and Rose Device quickly ran out of the way to clear a space for their landing.

Flare had both hands on Rainbow Butterfly’s face while she flew rapidly toward the surface. Rainbow Butterfly was struggling to break free of her hands and flapping his rainbow wings wildly, but his wings were in tatters, reduced to maybe a third of their original size.

Flare shrugged off his struggles and slammed him into the asphalt head first.

A normal human would have had their entire head destroyed, their skull shattered and their brains splattered over a wide area, but it was the asphalt that shattered here. Flare had already ascertained how sturdy Rainbow Butterfly was after their high-altitude fistfight.

Rainbow Butterfly lay at the bottom of the newly-formed crater, staring blankly up at Flare until he passed out.

Linda filled the silence with celebration.

“It’s the Thunderclaps! All four of them even! The Thunderclaps came to save us!”

She felt comfortable pulling out her phone and snapping photos of the four superheroes.

“So were you lying in wait here because you knew we were going to be attacked?”
“Well…” began Flare, but Star Thunder spoke over her and gave a firm explanation for Linda.

“We were ready to act because we had received reliable intel that the Enforcers of Evil were hiding in Ikebukuro to carry out a terrorist job! But we didn’t know who the target was, so I must apologize for failing to prevent this terrifying experience in advance!”

Flare could only smile bitterly at that.
(The truth is Rei has gotten really into Miracle Waffle and had all of us lined up so she could get all four versions of the one-per-person bonus that comes with a CD purchase here, but I guess that isn’t a very heroic answer.)

Flare herself was glad they didn’t have to reveal that all four of them had been wearing the official Miracle Waffle sweatshirts that Rei had bought them. They had the faces of the idols printed on the front and back.

“We will keep things under control here until the Metropolitan Police Department’s supercrimes division can-”

Flare stopped midsentence when a shadow fell on her.

She looked up to see a woman floating in the air.

The large woman was nearly 2m tall and wore a full-body costume resembling black armor. Only the head looking down at the Thunderclaps was exposed and the solemn beauty of her face gave her an inviolable aura.

The handle of a large axe glowed white on her back.

“Eh? North Wind!? What are you doing in Tokyo? Not to mention…”

North Wind was Hokkaido’s most famous superhero. Her strength came from a mystical power and she had some of the greatest skill and results in Japan. She had fought to protect that northern land for a full decade at this point. The Thunderclaps had previously met her when she came to Tokyo in pursuit of an Offbeat criminal.

She had changed her costume a few times over the past decade, but it had always been white. White was her iconic color, so one question naturally came to Flare’s mind.

“Why is your costume black?”

North Wind raised her muscular right hand in lieu of answering. The axe on her back moved all on its own to place its white handle in her hand.

A single swing of her arm sent the axe spinning through the air toward Flare. It moved too quickly for the ordinary human eye to follow, so it simply seemed to vanish for Linda.

The very next moment, Flare had caught the axe between her hands right in front of her. The incredible impact sent a tremor through her body and even her superhuman muscles and bones groaned and creaked.

“Ahh!”

Flare was shoved around a dozen meters back. Her feet tore two parallel grooves in the asphalt. She was astounded by the power of that sacred axe given to North Wind by an Ainu kamuy.

“North Wind, what do you think you’re- ugh.”

A hand reached past Flare’s head from behind and grabbed her face. Flare, the rest of her team, Linda, and the bodyguards had all failed to notice someone there until now. Whoever it was had completely hidden their presence from the human senses as well as Rose Device’s many sensors.

“Red Widow!” shouted Star Thunder. “Why!?”
Red Widow was a superhero known as the guardian of the Yokohama night. She was a master of the Woman Xian Fist, a form of super martial arts passed down by the women of an ethnic minority found deep in China.

As her name suggested, she was a widow who wore red Chinese clothing and a red eye mask for her costume, but her Chinese clothing and eye mask were both black now.

When this dark Red Widow removed her hand, Flare had something like a black starfish attached to her face, covering her eyes, nose, and mouth. It looked like a silly costume, but Flare had ceased moving while still holding North Wind’s axe.

Star Thunder told Rose Device to protect Linda’s group and then ran toward Flare. Ocelot ran forward at the same time.

Before the two of them made it even three steps, Flare and Red Widow vanished into thin air. So did North Wind in the sky above.

Flare’s teammates were left behind with nothing but unanswered questions.
