Curse Eater: Afterword
This is Aoi Muramasa.

I decided to become an author when I read a YA horror romance book put out by a certain publisher. I was hooked on the cool, grotesque, and thrilling story, so I started buying and reading everything by a specific author for the first time in my life.

And one fateful day, the new book put out by that author was an extremely erotic horror romance gun-action story!

I was hooked on that even more. I was so intent on writing something like that myself that I watched tons of horror movies, read all sorts of novels and history books, and even took a combat shooting class.

Ultimately, that one book I read led to the existence of this book, which is somewhat horror romance-ish and just a tad sexual.

And finally, I need to give a thousand thanks to Alto Seneka-san for the cool and cute character designs he made!
